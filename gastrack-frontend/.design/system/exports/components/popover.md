# Popover

## Visual

```
rounded-[6px] border border-border bg-popover shadow-md p-3 z-[1060]
```

Arrow: 8px rotated square with matching bg and border.

## Placement

top, bottom, left, right with auto-flip.

## Accessibility

- Trigger: `aria-expanded` and `aria-haspopup`
- Popover: `role="dialog"` or content-appropriate role
- Escape closes
- Click outside closes
